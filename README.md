### A little about me

I'm drawn to questions about **biodiversity**, **human–nature connections**, and the stories hidden in ecological data. I enjoy exploring those ideas through research, visualisation, and small interactive tools.

我关注 **生物多样性**、**人与自然的关系**，也喜欢用数据、可视化和一些小项目，把复杂的生态过程变得更直观一点。
